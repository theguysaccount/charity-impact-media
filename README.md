# Charity Impact Media: where the money goes

**Live:** https://theguysaccount.github.io/charity-impact-media/

Sourced spending breakdowns of every US 501(c)(3) public charity with $10 million or more in revenue, built from each charity's own IRS Form 990 filings: where $100 of spending went, where the money came from, the highest reported compensation, governance answers and up to seven years of trends. Every figure carries its filing year and links to its source. No ratings or grades.

Full documentation follows in this repository: `OPERATIONS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, `LAUNCH.md`, `STATE.md`.
